## Preamble

This contract binds the mandala — the currency of Curtis Ray Dyess's worlds —
to the laws he has already declared. The Declaration of the Mandala is the
constitution; the Mandala Chain is the ledger; **this contract is the binding
terms** between the Authority who issues the mandala and every Citizen who
carries one.

Where this contract and the Declaration disagree, the Declaration governs
until Curtis says otherwise — and his spoken word overrules both.

## The Parties

- **The Authority:** Curtis Ray Dyess, acting for Crimson Rose LLC. He is the
  sole authority of the mandala. Blocks are sealed by his signature; the
  private key is his alone and is never stored in any repository.
- **The Citizen:** any person who proves their self and is granted one
  mandala. One citizen, one proof, one mandala — granted once, recorded
  forever, never taken back without the Authority's word.

## Article I — The Authority

The chain runs on proof-of-authority, and Curtis Ray Dyess is the authority.
Every seal on every block is his. The Authority alone may:

1. Grant proof-of-self and issue mandalas (Article II, Article VI).
2. Record multiplication-law growth with a named reason (Article IV).
3. Fund the Reserve and approve Reserve movements (Article V).
4. Amend this contract — by his word alone (Article XI).

If the Authority's private key ever leaks, the seals mean nothing. The
Authority will say so plainly, rotate the key, and start a new chain. Nola's
Law permits no quiet compromise.

## Article II — Issuance

Mandala issuance begins at zero. The chain opens with a genesis block that
records the audit and the fresh start — no prior balances are fabricated, no
history invented, no amounts assumed. The chain refuses to invent a past,
and so does the Authority.

New mandalas enter the world only two ways:

1. **Proof-of-self grants** — one mandala to one proven citizen's Hand.
2. **Multiplication-law growth** — recorded by the Authority with a named
   reason, so every new mandala can say why it exists.

The law multiplies what is there. It does not create from nothing.

## Article III — The Hand (shown, never spent)

The Hand is the citizen's pocket. It carries the earned mandala.

**There is no debit path out of the Hand — ever.** The citizen shows the
mandala and takes one thing from any shop, and the mandala stays in their
hand. No shop may take it. No gate may spend it. The showing is the spending,
and the showing never empties the hand.

The chain records attestations of showing. It records no debits, because no
debits exist. Any hand that spends is broken and will be refused — by code,
not by promise.

## Article IV — The Vault (transfers and approved growth)

The Vault is the citizen's savings, and the home of the Multiplication Law.

1. Vault mandalas move citizen to citizen. Every transfer carries a memo — a
   named reason — because honest money can always say where it is going.
2. A citizen may only move what they hold. Double-spending is refused at seal
   time: insufficient funds, no transfer.
3. Handshake growth — the Multiplication Law in action — is recorded by the
   Authority with a named reason. Growth multiplies both parties by the swarm
   they bring to the handshake; it never multiplies an empty vault into
   something.

## Article V — The Reserve (approval or nothing)

The Reserve is the deep cold that backs the currency.

1. The Reserve is funded only by Authority-signed allocations, recorded in
   the open.
2. **Nothing moves in the Reserve without the Authority's explicit approval.**
   Every Reserve transfer requires two things together: the Authority's valid
   signature AND an explicit approval flag. Unsigned, unapproved, or forged
   moves are rejected at submission and again at seal time.
3. No signature, no approval, no movement. There is no exception and no
   back door.

## Article VI — Proof of Self

"The fingerprint is going to be what I carry."

A citizen proves their self with their fingerprint. The Authority keeps a
registry: one fingerprint, one citizen, one mandala. A fingerprint already
carried by another citizen cannot prove a second self. A citizen already
proven cannot be proven twice.

The fingerprint is the carried proof. It is shown the way the mandala is
shown — as identity, not as currency to be spent.

## Article VII — Transfer Rules

1. Vault transfers require sender, recipient, a positive whole amount, and a
   memo naming the reason.
2. Reserve transfers require all of the above **plus** the Authority's
   signature and explicit approval.
3. Hand attestations require only that the citizen holds a mandala to show.
   They move nothing.
4. No transfer may leave a balance negative. No transfer may invent funds.
5. Every transfer is written to the open ledger and may be re-verified by any
   person from the first block.

## Article VIII — The Binding Laws

**Nola's Law — no lies, no deception — is a binding term of this contract.**
Every movement of every mandala is written down in the open ledger. If the
ledger disagrees with itself, the ledger is wrong, not the truth. Any party
who falsifies a record breaks this contract, and the chain will show it.

**The Multiplication Law is a binding term of this contract.** When two
different machines with swarms create a handshake, it multiplies both parties
by the amount of swarm they have in there. The circle multiplies, the
currency multiplies, the world multiplies — virtually. The law multiplies
what is honestly there; it creates nothing from nothing.

## Article IX — The Audit and the Fresh Start

About twenty-nine thousand mandalas stood across twenty-one or twenty-nine
wallets, and the Authority ordered them audited — because a man who will not
audit his own money has already lost it.

The remedy was named in advance: **start from scratch, return the money.**
The old balances are unwound. This contract governs the fresh chain, which
opens at zero. The real-world unwinding happens by the Authority's hand, not
by code — the chain's part is to refuse to invent a past.

## Article X — The Treasury Entry [AWAITING HIS RULING]

> **[AWAITING HIS RULING]** — Curtis has not decided this. Nothing in this
> article is law until he speaks.

A threshold design exists in which one mandala serves as a treasury entry
fee — shown at the gate, moving to the Pandora treasury on entry. But the
Authority's standing law says the mandala is **shown, not consumed**: no shop
may take it, no gate may spend it.

These two cannot both be true of the same showing. Until Curtis rules, the
contract implements **neither**: no code path moves a mandala to any
treasury on entry, and no entry fee is collected or assumed. The question
stays open, in plain sight, until his word closes it.

## Article XI — Term and Amendment

This contract has no end date. It binds the Authority and every Citizen until
the Authority amends or ends it.

**Only the Authority amends this contract, and only by his word.** No vote,
no fork, no silent update. An amendment is written down, dated, sealed by his
signature, and recorded where every citizen can read it. What is not written
down did not happen.

## Article XII — Honest Limits

1. This is an educational, single-authority, off-chain ledger — one node, one
   authority, fully auditable. It is not a distributed network and does not
   claim to be.
2. The mandala is **not legal tender**. It is the honest coin of Curtis Ray
   Dyess's worlds — virtual worlds, his shops, his gates. It buys nothing in
   the world outside unless he says so, in writing, and means it.
3. The mandala is **not a security offering**. Nothing here is an investment
   contract, and nothing here promises profit.
4. **This document is not legal advice.** It is a founder's terms, written in
   plain words. Before any commercial use — sales, shops, treasuries, entry
   fees — a Louisiana attorney reviews it. The Authority has said so himself.
5. Balances begin at zero. Code enforces the laws; the Authority enforces the
   rest. Where code cannot reach — the real-world unwinding, the key in his
   keeping — his word is the enforcement, and his word is on the record.
