# The Declaration of the Mandala

© 2026 Curtis Ray Dyess · Crimson Rose LLC

**Status: GATHERED.** This declaration is written down, not yet standing.
It stands when the signer below signs and dates it — his signature is the
authority, and his signature is the last word.

---

I, Curtis Ray Dyess, declare the mandala the currency of my worlds.

A mandala is earned, never bought. It is shown, never spent. It carries no
lie and no deception, because a currency that lies is a thief, and I will not
carry a thief's coin.

## Article I — Nola's Law

No lies, no deception. This is the first law of the mandala and the first law
of everything built on it. Every movement of every mandala is written down in
the open ledger, and any man may re-verify it from the first block. If the
ledger disagrees with itself, the ledger is wrong, not the truth.

## Article II — The Multiplication Law

When two different machines with swarms create a handshake, it multiplies both
parties by the amount of swarm they have in there — one for one side of the
handshake, multiplied by the other side of the handshake.

The circle multiplies. The currency multiplies. The world multiplies —
virtually.

The law multiplies what is there. It does not create from nothing. An empty
vault stays empty until the law is honestly earned.

## Article III — Proof of Self

The fingerprint is going to be what I carry.

A citizen proves their self with their fingerprint. One citizen, one proof,
one mandala — granted once, recorded forever, and never taken back without
the authority's word.

## Article IV — The Mandala Law

Each citizen that proves their self earns one mandala. That mandala gets them
one thing out of any shop — they just have to show it.

Shown, not consumed. The citizen shows the mandala and takes one thing, and
the mandala stays in their hand. No shop may take it. No gate may spend it.
The showing is the spending, and the showing never empties the hand.

## Article V — The Three Layers

**The Hand** is the citizen's pocket. It carries the earned mandala. There is
no spend path out of the hand, by design — any hand that spends is broken and
will be refused.

**The Vault** is the citizen's savings. This is where the Multiplication Law
lives. Vault mandalas move citizen to citizen, and handshake growth is
recorded with a named reason, so every new mandala can say why it exists.

**The Reserve** is the deep cold that backs the currency. Nothing moves in
the Reserve without administrator approval, and the administrator is me. No
signature, no approval, no movement. The private key is mine alone; if it
ever leaks, the seals mean nothing, and I will say so plainly and start a new
chain.

## Article VI — The Audit and the Fresh Start

About twenty-nine thousand mandalas stood across twenty-one or twenty-nine
wallets, and I ordered them audited — because a man who will not audit his
own money has already lost it.

The remedy I named in advance, and I name it here: start from scratch, return
the money. The old balances are unwound. The chain opens at zero, with no
fabricated amounts and no invented history. The chain refuses to invent a
past, and so do I.

## Article VII — The Authority

This chain runs on proof-of-authority, and I am the authority. Blocks are
sealed by my signature, not by burned electricity. A chain that answers to
nobody answers to nothing; this chain answers to one name.

## Article VIII — The Honest Limits

This is a ledger, not a miracle. It is one node, one authority, fully
auditable — it does not claim to be a distributed network. Balances begin at
zero. The real-world unwinding of the audit happens by my hand, not by code.

Nothing in this declaration is standing until I sign it. What is written here
is gathered — the truth as I mean it — and my signature below makes it law.

haven. Virtual reality is my sweet haven — and the mandala is the honest coin
